Why does Thai text consume more tokens than English?

Thai script has no spaces between words and stacks vowels/tone marks vertically. Western-centric BPE tokenizers split Thai words into byte fragments (2–4 tokens per word), whereas Gemini 2.5 and Qwen 2.5 include dedicated multilingual vocabulary entries for whole Thai words.
